# 🎨 The Ultimate Font Pairing Guide & Typography Matrix
### Curated by [Mohammad Golamrabbii](https://linktr.ee/golamrabbii) for [English-Fonts-Mega-Archive](https://github.com/mohammadgolamrabbii/English-Fonts-Mega-Archive)

Great typography is the foundation of high-conversion digital experiences. Below are 10 battle-tested typography pairings combining header, body, and accent fonts, calibrated for specific design niches.

---

## 1. Modern Minimalist SaaS & Tech Startup
* **Header Font:** `Inter` (Font-Weight: 700 / Bold, Letter-Spacing: -0.02em)
* **Body Font:** `Roboto` (Font-Weight: 400 / Regular, Line-Height: 1.6)
* **Accent / Code:** `JetBrains Mono`
* **Vibe:** Clean, functional, Silicon Valley standard, accessible.
* **Best For:** Dashboards, SaaS landing pages, documentation, mobile apps.

---

## 2. Editorial Luxury, High Fashion & Boutique
* **Header Font:** `Playfair Display` (Font-Weight: 600 / SemiBold, Letter-Spacing: 0.01em)
* **Body Font:** `Montserrat` (Font-Weight: 300 / Light, Line-Height: 1.7)
* **Accent:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Italic)
* **Vibe:** Elegant, high-class, timeless, sophisticated.
* **Best For:** Luxury perfume, jewelry brands, wedding portfolios, fashion magazines.

---

## 3. High-CTR YouTube Thumbnail & Video Headline
* **Header Font:** `Bebas Neue` (Font-Weight: 900 / Heavy, Text-Transform: UPPERCASE)
* **Sub-Header:** `Poppins` (Font-Weight: 800 / ExtraBold, Letter-Spacing: 0.02em)
* **Vibe:** High-energy, maximum visibility, instant clickability.
* **Best For:** Video thumbnails, social media reels, sports posters, banner ads.

## 4. Cyberpunk, Gaming & AI Technology

## 5. Classic Literature & Book Publishing
